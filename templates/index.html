💡 How to Use

🎤 Speech to Text

  • Click "Start Recording" and speak clearly
  • Click "Stop Recording" when done
  • Your speech will be converted to text automatically

🌐 Translation

  • Use transcribed text or type your own
  • Select target language
  • Click "Translate Text"
  • Copy translated text with one click

🔊 Text to Speech

  • Enter text in any supported language
  • Select output language
  • Click "Convert to Speech"
  • Play the generated audio

⌨️ Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Ctrl + Enter - Translate text or convert to speech (when focused in textarea)
  • Escape - Stop recording
